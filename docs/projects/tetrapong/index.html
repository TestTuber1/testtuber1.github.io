How To Play Tetra Pong
• Bounce the ball past the other player!
• When the ball hits the back wall or a block, a new Tetris block appears.
• Blocks push your paddle closer to the middle line.
• If 3 matching blocks touch each other, they pop and disappear!
• Fill up a whole column of blocks from top to bottom to push the middle line toward the other player!
• Don't let your paddle touch the middle line, or you lose!
